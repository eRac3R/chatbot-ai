Frequently asked questions

The short version — ask the chat for anything more specific.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Account > Subscription > Cancel. It takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle and there are no cancellation fees.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently we only ship within the United States, but international shipping is on our roadmap. See the Shipping page for full details.
What if I don't like the coffee?
We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee on your first bag. Contact support within 30 days for a full refund — see the Contact page.
Can I send coffee as a gift?
Yes — the Gift a Bag plan is a single 12oz bag plus a welcome card for $18, one-time, no subscription required.
Can I switch plans later?
Anytime, from Account > Subscription. Changes apply from your next billing cycle.
