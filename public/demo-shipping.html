Shipping & Returns

Where your coffee goes, and what happens if it's wrong

The full policy — the chat can answer questions about this instantly too.

Shipping

🇺🇸

United States

Free shipping on every order, no minimum. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days from when your order roasts and ships.

🌍

International

We don't ship outside the US yet — it's on our roadmap. Sign up for updates via the Contact page and we'll let you know the moment it opens up.

📦

Subscription timing

Subscription orders roast and ship every 2 weeks on your billing date. You can skip, pause, or reschedule an upcoming shipment anytime from Account > Subscription.

Returns & guarantee

💯

First-bag guarantee

Don't love your first bag? We'll refund it in full — just contact support within 30 days of delivery, no questions asked.

🔁

Damaged or wrong order

If a shipment arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact support with your order number and we'll send a free replacement or refund.

🎁

Gift orders

Gift a Bag orders follow the same first-bag guarantee — the recipient (or you) can request a refund within 30 days if it's not a hit.
